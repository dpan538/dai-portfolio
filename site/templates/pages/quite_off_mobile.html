Quite Off by Dai Pan

Quite Off by Dai Pan is an unfinished film fragment and image-based work shot on Kodak Vision3 5203, 5207, and 5219. The project studies gaze, separation, motherhood, friendship, and the red-green motif through photography, writing, and visual art.

Year
2025
Medium
Kodak Vision3 film photography, writing, and unfinished film fragment
Art form
Film photography and image-based visual art
Location
New York / China
